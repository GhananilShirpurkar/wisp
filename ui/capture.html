About sharing on Linux ＋

On Wayland, your desktop opens a secure screen chooser. On X11, Choose a screen shares the primary display. Minimize this window to keep sharing. The worker needs capture enabled and a local vision model; sharing alone does not start analysis.

Screen images stay in memory. Text observations may be saved in the local workflow history.
